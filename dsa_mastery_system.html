Brain science behind this plan
Spaced repetition
Review after 3 days → 1 week → 2 weeks. Your brain consolidates memory during gaps, not during cramming.
Pattern chunking
Experts see chunks. Once you recognise "sliding window", a hundred problems collapse into one mental unit.
Sleep consolidation
Solve 2–3 problems before bed. Your hippocampus replays them during sleep — that's when real learning happens.
Teach-back method
After each problem write 1 line: "This works because…". Verbal encoding doubles retention.
Struggle first rule
Spend 20 min stuck before looking at hints. The struggle creates the neural groove. Easy solutions don't stick.

The 5-question framework
1
What does the input look like?
Array/string → Two Pointers, Sliding Window, Binary Search. Tree → DFS/BFS. Graph → BFS/DFS/Topo. Linked List → Fast/Slow.
2
What am I optimising for?
Min/Max → DP or Greedy. Count ways → DP. Find if exists → BFS/DFS. Top-K → Heap. Ranges → Merge Intervals.
3
Is the data sorted or sortable?
Yes → Binary Search or Two Pointers almost always. Sorting unlocks O(n log n) from O(n²) brute force.
4
Do I need all combos or just optimal?
All combinations/paths → Backtracking. Just optimal → Greedy or DP. Shortest path → BFS (unweighted).
5
Are subproblems overlapping?
Same smaller problem appears multiple times → DP. Local optimal leads to global optimal → Greedy (faster when applicable).
